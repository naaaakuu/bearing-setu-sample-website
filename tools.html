Housing
  • Fill the bearing completely, working grease between the balls or rollers.
  • Fill the free space in the housing 30 to 50% for normal speeds. Less at high speed, so the bearing does not run hot.

How often

Intervals depend on speed, temperature, load and dirt. Hotter, faster or dustier means more often. Use the machine maker’s schedule where it exists, and never mix grease thickeners without checking compatibility.
