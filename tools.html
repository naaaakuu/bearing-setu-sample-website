Interchange. One bearing, eight makers.

Enter a number from any brand. We read the size, sealing and clearance, then show how each maker writes the same bearing.

Equivalents share boundary dimensions and sealing type. For critical machines, also match the cage, internal clearance, lubricant and noise grade before you swap brands. Designations follow each maker’s usual pattern; the box is the final word.

Grease. How much, and which.

Relubrication quantity from the bearing’s outside diameter and width, with grease gun strokes you can count.

We look up D and B from ISO tables and our catalogue.

Most lever guns give about 1.5 g. Weigh ten strokes on a scale to check yours.

Gp = 0.005 × D × B, in grams. For relubrication from the side of the bearing.

First fill

Bearing
Housing
  • Fill the bearing completely, working grease between the balls or rollers.
  • Fill the free space in the housing 30 to 50% for normal speeds. Less at high speed, so the bearing does not run hot.

How often

Intervals depend on speed, temperature, load and dirt. Hotter, faster or dustier means more often. Use the machine maker’s schedule where it exists, and never mix grease thickeners without checking compatibility.
