    Is it genuine? Four checks before you fit it.

    A copied bearing can look right on the shelf and fail early in the machine. These checks take a few minutes.

    1. Packaging

      Sharp printing, the brand’s correct colours and fonts, and a label whose part number matches the bearing. Blurred print, spelling mistakes or a flimsy box are warning signs.

    2. Laser marking on the rings

      Brand, designation and origin should be crisp, evenly etched and centred. Marks that look stamped, smudged or uneven, or that differ from the box, need a second look.

    3. The brand’s own app

      SKF Authenticate lets you send photos of a part and its box to SKF for a check. Schaeffler OriginCheck scans the code on FAG and INA packaging. Both are free from the app stores.

    4. Batch details on our invoice

      Every line on a Bearingsetu GST invoice carries the batch number, so each part can be traced back through our stock records.

    Doubt a part you bought from us?

    Send photos of the part, the box and its label with your order number. A specialist checks them against our batch records and, where the brand offers one, its own verification service, and replies within 2 working days. If a part cannot be confirmed as genuine, we take it back and refund it in full, even after the 7-day return window.
