Choosing the right part

Where do I find the bearing number?

It is printed or laser-marked on the side face of the ring, for example 6205-2RS1. The first digits give the type and size. Use the part number decoder to read the rest of the letters.

What do ZZ, 2Z and 2RS mean?

ZZ or 2Z means a metal shield on both sides. 2RS (or DDU, LLU, 2RSR) means a rubber contact seal on both sides. Sealed bearings protect better against dust and moisture but allow a lower maximum speed than shielded ones.

What does C3 mean?

C3 is an internal clearance larger than normal. It is used where the bearing runs warm or has a tight fit. If you are replacing a bearing, match the clearance that was marked on the old one.

Can I use another brand instead of the one I have?

Bearings of the same basic number and suffix are dimensionally interchangeable between makers. Use the equivalent parts finder to see designations. Confirm suitability for your application, especially for special clearances, cages and high speeds.

How do I measure a V-belt?

Use the number printed on the belt if you can read it. Otherwise measure the top width and height to find the section (A, B, C, SPA, SPB…), then the length. The belt length calculator helps when the belt is missing.

How does an oil seal size work?

Oil seals are named shaft diameter × housing bore × width, for example 25×52×7. Measure those three on the old seal or the machine and search them in that order, for example 25x52x7.

How do quotations work?

Add parts to your quote list and send the request. We reply with price, availability and freight for your quantities and delivery location. Nothing is charged by sending a request.
