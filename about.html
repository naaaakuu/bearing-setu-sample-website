Setu means bridge.

Between the brands and stockists who make and hold parts, and the plants that need them on time. That is the whole idea.

The part exists. Getting it to the machine is the hard bit.

A bearing seizes on a Saturday. The right replacement sits on some stockist’s shelf, but the plant finds out too late, pays too much or gets a box nobody can vouch for. Bearingsetu exists to close that gap.

We list what we can actually ship, price it for the volumes plants really buy, and put people who know part numbers on the other end of every question.

  1. Source.

    We buy through brand channels and established stockists, record batch details, and only list a part once we know where it comes from.

  2. Supply.

    Parts are picked and packed in Pune. Order by 4 pm, Monday to Saturday, and in-stock parts leave the same day with a GST invoice in the box.

  3. Grow.

    Tier prices, saved lists and 30-day credit for verified business accounts help a plant buy steadily, not in a panic.

Is FAG 6205-2RSR-C3 the same as SKF 6205-2RS1/C3?

Yes. Same 25 × 52 × 15 mm size, rubber seals on both sides and C3 clearance. FAG writes the seals as 2RSR. Both are in stock in Pune.
