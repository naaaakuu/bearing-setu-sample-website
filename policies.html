Shipping and delivery

Payments and credit

Payment terms are confirmed in your quotation, for example bank transfer against a proforma invoice. We do not take card details on this website.

Credit is not available on every account. It applies only to business accounts for which our team has enabled it, within a limit and payment period set for that account. Credit can be changed or withdrawn, and you are told by email when that happens.

Returns and cancellations

Terms for returns and cancellations are agreed for each order and appear on the quotation or invoice. Please contact us before sending anything back.

Terms of use

  • Prices and availability are confirmed in a written quotation and apply for the validity period stated on it. Sending a quotation request does not create an order.
  • An order is accepted when we confirm it. GST is charged as applicable and shown on the quotation and invoice.
  • Sizes, equivalent designations and specifications on this website are a guide to help you choose. Confirm that a part suits your application and check the manufacturer's datasheet for critical duties.
  • Product photographs show the type of part. The brand markings and packaging of the part supplied may differ.
  • Terms specific to your order appear on your quotation and invoice and take precedence over this summary.

Privacy

What we collect

  • Quotation requests: your name, company, GSTIN (if you enter it), phone, email, delivery pincode, notes and the parts you list. We use them to reply to your request and to prepare quotations and invoices.
  • Business accounts (where switched on): the registration details above, a securely hashed password, your quotations and orders, and credit information if your account has been approved for it.

What stays on your device

Your browser stores your quote list, display preferences, recent searches and, when you sign in, a sign-in token. Nothing else is stored, and this website does not use advertising or analytics cookies.

Third parties

If you upload an Excel file in Quick order, the Excel reader is loaded from a public software library (cdnjs). Email and WhatsApp links open your own apps and are subject to those services' terms.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete your details by contacting us. We keep records for as long as needed to deal with your request or order and to meet legal and accounting requirements.
