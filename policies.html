Terms of use

By using bearingsetu.com you agree to these terms. They apply to everyone who browses, asks for a quote or places an order, whether as an individual or on behalf of a business.

Prices and listings

  • Prices are per unit in Indian rupees and exclude GST unless a page says otherwise.
  • Tier prices apply automatically when the quantity of a single part reaches the tier.
  • If a price or stock count is listed in error, we will tell you before dispatch and you may cancel without charge.

Product information

Photos show the type of part. Brand markings, packaging and colour on the part you receive can differ. Dimensions, load ratings and speeds are typical catalogue values; check the maker’s datasheet for critical applications.

Brands

Brand names and trademarks belong to their owners. Listing a brand does not mean we are its appointed distributor. We source through brand channels and established stockists, and print batch details on the invoice where the maker provides them.

Equivalents and tools

The part number decoder, interchange table and calculators are guides. They follow common published conventions, but a maker’s own catalogue is the final reference. Ask a specialist before swapping parts on safety-critical machines.

Accounts and quotes

Quotes are valid for 7 days unless stated. Business accounts are for registered businesses with a valid GSTIN. Keep your sign-in details private; you are responsible for orders placed from your account.

These terms are governed by the laws of India, and courts in Pune, Maharashtra have jurisdiction.

Privacy

We collect only what we need to quote, deliver and invoice your order, and we never sell your details.

What this sample stores

In this sample store, everything you do stays in your own browser’s local storage: your bag, saved items, recent searches, preferences, sample orders, quote requests and forms you send. Nothing is sent to a server, and clearing it here removes it for good.

Good to know

  • Greases and oils travel by road. Aerosols are never sent by air.
  • An e-way bill is raised for consignments above ₹50,000, as the law requires.
  • Check the box on delivery. Note any visible damage on the courier’s receipt and tell us within 48 hours.

Payments and credit

GST

GST at 18% applies to every part we sell. Because we ship from Maharashtra, orders delivered within Maharashtra carry CGST 9% and SGST 9%. Orders delivered to any other state carry IGST 18%. Add your GSTIN at checkout to claim input tax credit.

Ways to pay

  • UPI, net banking and cards through a secure payment page of a regulated payment provider.
  • Bank transfer against a proforma invoice for larger orders.
  • 30-day credit for verified business accounts.

30-day credit

Business accounts can apply for credit once the GSTIN is verified. Approved accounts receive a credit limit and pay each invoice within 30 days of the invoice date. How credit works

Returns and refunds

You can return unused parts in their original, unopened packing within 7 days of delivery.

  1. Start a return. Use Returns with your order number, or email support@bearingsetu.com.
  2. Send it back. We arrange the pickup. If we sent the wrong or a damaged part, we pay for the return.
  3. Get your refund. Once the part is checked, we refund to the original payment method, or as a credit note for business accounts.

What cannot be returned

  • Parts that have been fitted, greased, heated or damaged after delivery.
  • Greases and oils once the seal is broken.
  • Chains cut to length, belts made to length and seals made to drawing.

Wrong or damaged parts must be reported within 48 hours of delivery, with a photo of the part and the label.
