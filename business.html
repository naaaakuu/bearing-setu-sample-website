Questions, answered.

Still unsure? Request a call-back or write to orders@bearingsetu.com.

What documents do I need?

To open the account: your GSTIN, a work email and a mobile number. For 30-day credit we also ask for the last six months of GST returns (GSTR-3B), the business PAN and two trade references from suppliers who give you credit today. We request these by email after the GSTIN check, so there is nothing to attach on this page.

How is my credit limit set?

Limits run from ₹50,000 to ₹10 lakh. We look at the turnover in your GST returns, how regularly you file and what your trade references tell us. Limits are reviewed every quarter, and you can ask for an increase after three months of on-time payments.

Who in my team can log in?

Add as many colleagues as you need and give each a role: buyer, approver or admin. Buyers can place orders up to their own limit. Anything above it goes to an approver before it is placed, and every order shows who raised and approved it.

How can we pay?

Credit invoices are paid by NEFT, RTGS or UPI within 30 days of the invoice date. Prepaid orders can be paid by UPI, net banking or card at checkout. Each payment is matched to its invoice in your account, so your accounts team can reconcile in one place.

What if we miss a due date?

We remind you seven days before and on the due date. If an invoice goes overdue, new credit orders pause until it is cleared, and you can still order with prepaid payment in the meantime. Repeated delays can lower your limit at the next quarterly review.
