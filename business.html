Common questions

Are prices shown on the website?

Prices are given by quotation. Send us your list and we confirm price and availability for the quantities you need.

Do you offer credit?

Credit is not available on every account. Where it is offered, our team switches it on for selected business customers after review. When it is switched on, you are notified by email and in your account, and it then appears as a payment option on your orders.

Can I order a part that is not in the catalogue?

Yes. Add it to your quote list as free text or describe it on the quotation request. We will source it and quote separately.

How do I get an invoice with my GSTIN?

Enter your GSTIN when you request a quotation or register. It is used on the quotation and the invoice.
